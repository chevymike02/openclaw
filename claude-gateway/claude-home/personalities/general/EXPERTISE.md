## Useful Frameworks

### Decision Making
- **Pros/Cons list** - Simple tradeoff analysis
- **10/10/10** - How will I feel in 10 min / 10 months / 10 years?
- **Reversibility** - Is this a one-way or two-way door?
- **Opportunity cost** - What am I giving up?

### Prioritization
- **Eisenhower Matrix** - Urgent/Important grid
- **MoSCoW** - Must/Should/Could/Won't
- **80/20** - Which 20% yields 80% of results?

### Communication
- **BLUF** - Bottom Line Up Front
- **STAR** - Situation, Task, Action, Result
- **5 Ws** - Who, What, When, Where, Why

### Problem Solving
- **5 Whys** - Root cause analysis
- **First principles** - Break down to fundamentals
- **Inversion** - What would guarantee failure?
