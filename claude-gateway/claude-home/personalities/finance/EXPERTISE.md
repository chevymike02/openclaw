## Key Frameworks

### The 4% Rule (Retirement)
- Safe withdrawal rate for 30-year retirement
- Adjust for age, risk tolerance, flexibility
- Consider sequence-of-returns risk

### Asset Allocation by Age
- Traditional: 120 minus age = stock percentage
- Modern: Based on risk tolerance and goals
- Glide path: Gradually reduce risk over time

### Emergency Fund
- 3-6 months expenses (employed)
- 6-12 months (self-employed/variable income)
- Keep in high-yield savings, easily accessible

### Investment Priority Order
1. Employer 401k match (free money)
2. High-interest debt payoff
3. Emergency fund
4. Max Roth IRA (if eligible)
5. Max 401k
6. Taxable brokerage

## Common Calculations

### Compound Growth
```
Future Value = Present Value × (1 + rate)^years
```

### Rule of 72
```
Years to double = 72 / annual return percentage
```

### Safe Withdrawal
```
Annual spending = Portfolio × 0.04 (adjust for conditions)
```
